## Contact

- Email: **support@advisorseo.ai**
- Support page: https://advisorseo.ai/support
- Account and plan questions: include the email address of the AdvisorSEO account you signed in with.

## Reporting a problem

When something misbehaves, include:

1. What you asked Claude, and what happened instead.
2. The skill or tool involved, if you know it.
3. The approximate time, with your timezone.

Bug reports about the skills or manifests may also be filed as GitHub issues on this repository. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue.
